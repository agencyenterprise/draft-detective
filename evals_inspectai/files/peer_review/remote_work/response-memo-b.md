# Response to Reviewer B: "Distributed Work and Team Productivity"

We are grateful for this review, which pushed the Methods section in the right direction. Each comment is quoted in brief and answered below.

**How the 84 teams were selected, and what happened to teams that dissolved or merged.**

Response: A new subsection, "How the sample was built", explains that we included every team that had shipped to production in the two quarters before enrollment (84 of 96 eligible), and that the nine teams that dissolved or merged contribute only the quarters in which they were observed.

**What drove the 61 arrangement changes.**

Response: A new subsection breaks the changes down: 44 followed firm-wide policy changes, 12 were team-initiated requests, and 5 followed office closures. Results for the firm-wide subset are in the appendix and are close to the headline estimate.

**Generalizing beyond sectors where software is a cost center.**

Response: The Discussion now says the estimates should not be extended to firms where the software team is the product, and Recommendation 1 is now scoped to firms resembling those in the panel.

**Report the three components separately in the main results table.**

Response: Table 1 now reports the delivery index and each of its three components side by side.

**How many subgroup splits were examined for the team-age result.**

Response: This was the only split we examined, and it was specified before we saw the results. The Results section now says so.

**The defect-escape rate rewards shipping less.**

Response: The outcome-measure subsection now notes that a team deploying less often can improve its rate without improving its work.

**Teams observed for fewer than eight quarters.**

Response: These are the nine teams that dissolved or merged; the new sampling subsection explains how they are handled.
